Common Issues I've Advised On

The situations where I tend to be most useful.

We're set on Meta, Google, and Apple Search. Which channels do we tackle next?

Core channels are running well, but you know there's more room to grow. I help evaluate the channel landscape — CTV, DSPs, Snap, TikTok, Reddit, partnerships — based on your vertical, audience, and LTV profile, and build the testing plan to expand without cannibalizing what's already working.

How do we scale paid acquisition when we can't see most of our iOS spend clearly?

iOS 14.5+ broke the visibility most teams were used to. I help build the measurement stack — SKAdNetwork, MMPs, incrementality testing, and modeled attribution — that lets you make confident budget decisions even without deterministic data on every install.

Growth is slowing. We've tried contractors, agencies, and in-house — same results every time.

When the obvious moves stop working, the constraint usually isn't the vendor — it's the strategy, the measurement, or the creative. I dig into what's actually happening: where spend is going, what the data says vs. what it appears to say, and what assumptions have gone unchallenged for too long.

How do we tackle measurement and attribution as we scale across more channels?

More channels means more conflicting data. Platform reporting overcounts. MMPs undercount. Finance sees something different entirely. I help teams build a measurement framework that reconciles these sources, clarifies what's actually driving growth, and gives you a reliable foundation for budget allocation decisions.

We have no problem getting new users — but we're losing them fast after install.

Acquisition is only half the equation. If onboarding is leaky, install-to-paid conversion is flat, or churn is erasing what paid acquisition builds, that's where I focus. I help identify the drop-off points, prioritize fixes, and connect the acquisition and product teams around the same funnel metrics.

We need to improve our operational efficiency, optimization frameworks and creative production and testing system to reduce our paid CAC.

High CAC is usually a systems problem — not just a bidding problem. I help tighten the feedback loops between creative, data, and media buying, build structured testing frameworks that compound over time, and streamline how your team produces, evaluates, and iterates on creative. The result is a lower cost to acquire, not just a busier team.

How do we build an integrated strategy where acquisition, retention, and lifecycle work together?

When acquisition, lifecycle, and product teams optimize in silos, they often undermine each other. I help design the connective tissue — shared metrics, audience segmentation, messaging alignment, and feedback loops — that turn separate functions into a single growth system.

How do I get more from my agency — or should I bring it in-house? How do I build the team?

The agency vs. in-house question depends on your stage, budget, and goals — and the honest answer is usually somewhere in between. I help audit current agency performance, identify where in-house ownership creates the most leverage, and build out the hiring plan, role definitions, and internal structure to make the transition work.

TikTok

Creative Testing & Diversification

Leveraging the platform's breadth of trending organic content for paid.

Creative StrategyMeta & Google UACA/B TestingPaid Acquisition

When I joined TikTok's U.S. Growth team, acquisition relied almost entirely on viral entertainment content (dance, comedy, sports) because those creatives consistently hit aggressive daily new user targets at ROAS above 100%. That consistency created a hidden problem: little organizational incentive to test new creative, even as TikTok's broader content ecosystem went largely untapped.

I built a structured creative experimentation framework over two quarters. Working with a data analytics partner, I created reporting that surfaced emerging trends across DIY, parenting, recipes, and fashion. Tests ran in isolated ad accounts to prevent established creative from dominating delivery. Initial results showed higher CPIs for diversified content, but extending analysis to D7-D14 retention curves reversed the picture. Higher-retention cohorts delivered ROAS above 100% despite higher CPIs, unlocking meaningful incremental scale.

About Me & My Work

I've spent the better part of my career as an operator, building, scaling, and at times rebuilding growth programs across gaming, entertainment, social media, fintech, and consumer AI, at companies ranging from seed-stage startups to post-IPO global brands.

Before growth, I worked in finance as a product manager in private wealth, where I spent a lot of time trying to understand what actually drives businesses: the macro trends shaping markets, the capital chasing them, and the underlying unit economics separating companies that scale from ones that stall. That analytical lens followed me when I eventually made the leap into mobile. I was drawn in by the convergence of mobile-first consumer behavior, social media as instant global distribution, and the rapidly intensifying competition for people's attention that consumer apps were winning.

That decision led me to several in-house and agency-side growth roles at top-grossing, category-leading startups and even a few that ultimately failed. Every experience taught me new lessons on the controllable factors that drive whether a startup makes it to IPO. This breadth of experience gave me direct exposure to growth at every stage: soft-launch and GTM planning for seed-stage products, 0-to-1 acquisition for early-stage startups, scaling through the messy middle, to managing 8-figure monthly budgets for late-stage and post-IPO brands.

Along the way I built hands-on expertise across the adjacent functions that help drive revenue growth: ASO, CRO, growth partnerships, lifecycle marketing, and the foundational frameworks for attribution, measurement, creative testing, and analytics. Over time, the problems that held my attention became broader than optimizing and scaling paid channels.

Paid acquisition is scaling but revenue and margins are still stagnant. Why?

We've raised another round. Where should we invest, in acquisition or somewhere else in the funnel?

We have no problem getting installs, but we're pouring water into a leaky bucket. How do we fix what's upstream?

We have gaps in our analytics and no clear view of how our marketing actually performs. Where do we start?

Our creative and messaging strategy needs a full rethink, but I can't pinpoint exactly what's broken.

How do we get acquisition, lifecycle, and content working together instead of operating in silos?

I've watched promising companies stall at the same inflection points, make the same avoidable mistakes, and invest in the wrong channels and parts of funnel at the wrong time. Not because they lacked ambition, but because they lacked the perspective that only comes from experience. It's why I now work with founders and growth teams to build the strategies, systems, and operational foundations that give them a real shot at the next stage.
